# Rule 12

The Rule 12 suite evaluates `decide_rule12_motion`.  Its eighteen fixtures cover pleading defects, jurisdiction and standing grounds, allegations accepted at the pleading stage, amendment, and prejudice.  The scorer checks disposition, ground-specific fields, leave to amend, prejudice, reason tags, invalid payloads, and Lean acceptance.
